# Abstract (249 words; same text as report/saboot-final-v2.pdf)

**Saboot: How Much of Hindi News About Government Schemes Can Be Bound to Official Numbers, and Does an LLM Verifier Know When It Can't?**

A Hindi newspaper reported that "about 10 crore" farmers received the 24th PM-KISAN instalment. The official figure was 9.34 crore, so the claim was 7.1% too high. We collected 102 such numerical claims about six central schemes from four Hindi outlets. We asked how many can be tied to a dated official statistic, and whether an LLM verifier can tell when they cannot. Only 45.1% of claims (46/102) could be bound to an official statistic, and 24.5% cleanly. National claims were bound far more often than state claims (69.2% against 20.0%). Of the 25 cleanly bound claims, 88.0% matched. We then tested a free LLM verifier, gemini-3.5-flash-lite, on every claim in Hindi and English. Without evidence, it answered "cannot verify" in 92.2% of its 204 responses. Its abstention rate was similar on cleanly bound claims (92.0%) and unbindable ones (93.8%). Abstention was not the only problem: when it committed, it was usually wrong, and none of its cited sources showed its figure; one cited PIB release is about a Holi special train. Given the official excerpt, false abstention on cleanly bound claims fell from 92.0% to 0.0%, and 97.7% of answers on matching claims were MATCH. But the verifier never labelled an ambiguous claim AMBIGUOUS (0 of 42 answers), and it called 13.6% of near-miss answers a mismatch. Its overall agreement (68.8%) stayed below a simple excerpt-type baseline (76.2%). Verifiers in this domain should be judged on whether their answers follow the evidence, not on how often they abstain.
